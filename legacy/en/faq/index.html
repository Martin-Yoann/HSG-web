FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How are Happy Global Service and Happy Global Inc. related?

Happy Global Service is the service brand of Happy Global Inc. All services are contracted, coordinated and delivered by Happy Global Inc.

What does Happy Global Service do?

For brand companies entering the U.S. market, we provide six service lines: retail and channel entry, content and localized marketing, recruiting and executive search, tax and listing-structure planning, cross-border logistics and fulfillment, and HR operations and employment compliance. We are headquartered in Dallas, Texas, with a base in Los Angeles, and all services are delivered under Happy Global Inc.

Are you a consulting firm?

No. We have our headquarters, content studio and R&D lab in Dallas, a base in Los Angeles and four warehouse locations that fulfill close to the customer, and we operate brands of our own. Every service is delivered by our own team in the U.S.

Which companies do you work with?

Companies with products and brand value of their own that plan to operate in the U.S. market for the long term. Size is not the deciding factor.

We do not have a U.S. entity yet. Can we still talk?

Yes. Most companies approach us before establishing a U.S. entity, and we can advise on sequencing. If you need to hire U.S. employees first, an Employer of Record (EOR) arrangement can be considered.

Can you guarantee placement with a specific retailer?

No. We provide channel assessment, preparation, introductions and follow-up; the decision rests with the retailer.

Do you provide legal or tax advice?

Legal, tax, securities and immigration opinions are issued by licensed professionals. We introduce the lawyers and accountants we work with and coordinate the work to completion.

Do you work in English and Chinese?

Yes. Our U.S. team operates locally, and our China team can work directly with your headquarters in Chinese.
