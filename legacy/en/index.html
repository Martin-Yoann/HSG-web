Our U.S. locations

Headquarters and R&D in Dallas, brand showcase in Los Angeles

Dallas is our headquarters: our team works, produces content and receives clients here, and our PulseOn brand is based here. The R&D lab for PulseOn and Glimboo is located at our headquarters, and a larger R&D center is under construction. Los Angeles is the first stop in the U.S. for many Chinese brands; our base there focuses on brand showcasing and partner relations. Warehousing is handled by four locations that fulfill close to the customer. Visitors are welcome.
