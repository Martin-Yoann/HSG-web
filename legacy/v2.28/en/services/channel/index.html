Sales Channel Entry

Channel entry is the central step in bringing a brand to the U.S. market. We completed entry into mainstream U.S. offline and online channels with brands of our own, and we serve brand companies on that basis.

Sales Channel EntryContent & Localized MarketingRecruiting & HeadhuntingFinance & StructureLogistics & WarehousingHR & Employment

When it applies

  1. Major U.S. retailers expect suppliers to have an accountable, traceable presence in the country.
  2. Labels, ingredients and packaging must meet U.S. regulations; adjustments take time.
  3. Channel margin structures and promotional calendars shape pricing and negotiation.

What we provide

  • Channel assessment and entry planning
  • Compliance and labeling adjustments (formal review by licensed firms)
  • Introductions to online and retail channels through our own distribution relationships

Before channels come consumers. What our own brands learn in the U.S. market informs every channel recommendation.
