Our U.S. locations

Headquarters and R&D in Dallas, brand showcase in Los Angeles

Dallas is our headquarters: our team works, produces content and receives clients here, and our PulseOn brand is based here. The R&D lab for PulseOn and Glimboo is located at our headquarters, and a larger R&D center is under construction. Los Angeles is the first stop in the U.S. for many Chinese brands; our base there focuses on brand showcasing and partner relations. Warehousing centers on the Dallas warehouse operated by Happy Global Logistics, with coordinated locations in Los Angeles, Chicago and Phoenix fulfilling close to the customer. Visitors are welcome.
